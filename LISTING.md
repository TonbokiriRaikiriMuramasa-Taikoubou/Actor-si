# Agensi listing draft — minutes-writer

- **Title:** Minutes Writer
- **Short description:** Turns meeting transcripts or notes into structured minutes with decisions, action items, owners, due dates, and open questions. Use when you need reliable minutes from meeting material.
- **Category suggestion:** Productivity / Writing
- **Tags:** meeting minutes, action items, decisions, notes
- **Compatible agents:** Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and other Agent Skills-compatible hosts (verify host-specific support)
- **External communication:** None by the skill itself. The skill only processes the content supplied in the host conversation; the host's own service may have separate data handling.
- **Example input:** “Turn these notes into minutes: launch approved; Kai owns QA by Friday; budget question remains open.”
- **Example output:** Summary, decisions, action items (owner, due date, deliverable), and open questions; unknown details remain marked for confirmation.
